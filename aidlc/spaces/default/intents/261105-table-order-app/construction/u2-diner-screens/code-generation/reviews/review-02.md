### Summary

One Critical defect (SSE event replay is silently broken because the client sends `lastEventId` as a query param but the server reads it as an HTTP header), two Major spec gaps (ORDER_PLACED not handled in My Orders; successful order does not switch tab; dropped_lines partial success not handled — counted as two separate issues), and three Minor findings (backoff sequence, missing reconnecting banner, takeover text). The Critical defect means that the SSE replay feature advertised in FR4.2 and the functional spec is non-functional in production, and the three Major gaps mean the success flow deviates from the specified behaviour in ways the team will notice during the walkthrough.
