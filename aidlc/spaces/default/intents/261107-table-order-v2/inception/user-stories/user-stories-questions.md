# User Stories Questions

This is the story plan for Table Order v2. Stories follow "As a [persona], I want [goal], so that [benefit]", pass the INVEST checks (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable), carry Given/When/Then acceptance criteria, and take their Must / Should / Could priority from the scope document. Story priorities inform the build order in Delivery Planning. They are built from the requirements in `inception/requirements-analysis/requirements.md` (19 feature groups, 8 non-functional requirements).

## Q1. [Product owner] Which personas should the stories be written for?

A persona is a person the story is about. This decides how many sets of screens and goals the stories cover.

A. Three personas: the Diner, the Kitchen staff (cooks at the board and stations), and the Front-of-house staff (waiters and the person at the till, who also manage the menu, specials and sales) (Recommended)
B. Two personas: the Diner and the Staff, with kitchen and front-of-house together
C. Four personas: the Diner, the Kitchen staff, the Front-of-house staff and a Restaurant manager (who owns the menu, specials, sales and ratings)
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. Three personas: Diner, Kitchen staff, Front-of-house staff

## Q2. [Product owner] How big should each story be?

Smaller stories are easier to test and to drop if time runs out; fewer, larger stories are quicker to read.

A. One story per feature, so about 20 stories, each with several Given/When/Then criteria (Recommended)
B. Split each feature by who does what (for example, the diner's half and the staff's half), so about 35 stories
C. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. One story per feature

## Q3. [Product owner] How should the stories be grouped in the document?

Grouping only changes how the document reads; every story still traces to a requirement.

A. By feature, in the order of the scope document (Must, then Should, then Could) (Recommended)
B. By persona, so each person's stories sit together
C. By step of the diner's visit (arrive, choose, order, prepare, wait and eat, pay and close, review)
D. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. By feature
