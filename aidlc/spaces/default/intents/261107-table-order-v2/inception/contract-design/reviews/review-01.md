### Summary

The design can be built in most respects: the parallel Units meet only through the listed contracts, v1 is kept, and the schema upgrade is safe. Seven open Major items remain before U6 and U7 can be built without guessing: a block that does not parse as YAML, no staff route to read hidden menu items, a presence design that cannot work with `EventSource`, no client rule for an expired staff session, an undecided estimate formula, a rating lock with no route, and an unspecified station-done transition.
