# Refined Mockups Questions

These questions come from the stories (`inception/user-stories/stories.md`), the requirements and the scope you approved. The mockups are high-fidelity HTML pages, one per screen, with an `index.html` that links them all. v1's rules for diner screens stay: a bottom tab bar (Menu, Cart, My orders) with the Cart count, "View cart" and "Place order" just above it, sticky category pills, a "+ Add" pill with a 44 px touch area, frosted bars, and all motion off for reduced motion. Everything else I decided is listed as an assumption in `mockups.md`.

## Q1. [Designer] What should the new design system look like?

You chose the new look for every screen and Dark mode that follows the device (so v1's current dark look becomes the dark theme). This decides the colours and type that every screen shares.

A. Evolve v1: keep the amber accent and the dark theme as they are, add a matching light theme, and tidy the type, spacing and shadows into one scale (Recommended)
B. A fresh brand colour (a green or teal accent) in both themes; v1's amber is retired
C. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. Evolve v1

## Q2. [Designer] How do staff move between the Kitchen Board and the new staff pages (Floor map, Menu, Specials, Sales, QR codes)?

v1 had no navigation on staff screens. Seven staff pages need one.

A. A side navigation on wide screens that becomes a top bar with a menu button on narrow screens (Recommended)
B. A bottom tab bar like the diner's, with the most-used pages and a "More" tab
C. Not yet defined
X. Other (please specify)

[Answer]: A. Side navigation
