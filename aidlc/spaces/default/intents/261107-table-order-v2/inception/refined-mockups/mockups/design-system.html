Rules that stay from v1

  • Bottom tab bar for the diner (Menu, Cart with count, My orders), never at the top
  • Sticky category pills; the current one is filled
  • 44 px touch areas (the "+ Add" pill looks 32 px but has a 44 px touch area)
  • Frosted bars, rounded cards, soft shadows, aligned tabular prices
  • All motion stops when the device asks for reduced motion; focus is always visible
