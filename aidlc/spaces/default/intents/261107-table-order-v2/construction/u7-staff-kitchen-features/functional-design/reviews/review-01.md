### Summary

U7's design is complete and buildable on what U5 provides: every acceptance criterion is covered, no schema change or diner-file edit is needed, and U6 and U7 do not touch the same files. The one point to settle before approving is the double-press behaviour of a station done on an order that has just completed (one Major); the rest are small gaps in wording and edge rules.
