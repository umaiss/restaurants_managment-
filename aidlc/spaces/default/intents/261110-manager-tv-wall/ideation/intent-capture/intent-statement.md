# Intent Statement — Manager TV Wall

## Problem Statement

- The manager and staff at the pass cannot see the whole service at a glance. Today they have to open several separate staff pages on a phone or tablet to find out how service is going. [Q1]
- The request is a staff-only, full-screen view on a big screen at the pass. It shows live orders, today's takings so far, the busiest table and the top dish, and it updates live. [desc]
- It adds to the finished v1 and v2 app without changing how either of them behaves. [desc]
- Revenue must not be visible where diners can see it, so the view needs a setting that hides the takings. [desc]

## Target Customer

- The main viewers are the manager and the front-of-house staff working at the pass. [Q2]
- They benefit by reading the state of service from one screen instead of opening several staff pages on a handheld device. [Q1] [Q2]

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Time to read the state of service | From across the pass, a viewer can read the open orders, the oldest wait, today's takings, the busiest table and the top dish in under 5 seconds | [Q3] |
| Hands-free use | None of the above needs anyone to touch a device | [Q3] |
| Freshness | The screen shows any change within 2 seconds of it happening | [Q3] |

## Initiative Trigger

- The Manager TV wall is the X-factor idea from the v2 brief that v2 did not pick. The team wants it now as a follow-up to the finished app. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected plan: `dpl-workshop`. [scope]
- In this first version: a staff-only, full-screen, live-updating view of live orders, today's takings so far, the busiest table and the top dish, with a setting that hides the takings. [desc]
- Left out of this first version: buttons or touch on the TV, because it only displays information. [Q6]
- Left out of this first version: history and date ranges, because it shows today only. [Q6]
